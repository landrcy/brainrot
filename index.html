📜 Lore

Born when a lightning bolt hit an old olive tree during Ramadan, Tung Tung woke up already holding the bat. His first word was tung. His second word was also tung. His ninth word was sahur, and the village has been awake ever since.

He was elected Chair of the Night Council unopposed, because nobody wanted to tell him no at 3 AM.
